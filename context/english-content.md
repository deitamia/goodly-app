Version: 0.3 · 2026-10-03.

The prose below is accepted copy, preserved verbatim. Stable content-key suggestions are planning aids, not a chosen runtime schema. Missing copy remains pending; do not label generated drafts as user-approved.

## About the idea

Suggested key: `home.about_idea`

Our mission is to support parents and caregivers of children with disabilities by connecting the products they create and the services they offer with customers who value and need them.

We help them reach people locally and around the world, create new opportunities, build meaningful connections, and grow their income through their own work.

The platform is free to use, with no commissions, selling fees, or charges for sellers or buyers.

## Who can become a seller? — homepage

Suggested key: `home.seller_eligibility`

Parents and caregivers who care for a child with disabilities and offer products or services they create or provide themselves can apply to become sellers on the platform.

These products or services may be a primary or additional source of income for them and their household.

## Accepted error and moderation messages

This nickname is already taken.

This category is no longer available. Please select an active category.

Your listing “Listing name” was blocked because it does not comply with the site rules.

`Listing name` is the runtime listing-title placeholder in the last sentence. Define a safe placeholder mechanism later rather than using unrestricted template execution. Blocking message goes only to Dashboard, no email.
