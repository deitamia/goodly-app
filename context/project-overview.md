# Project overview

Version: 0.3 · 2026-10-03 · Accepted product direction.

## Identity and mission

Working brand: **Goodly**. Pavel may change the brand later. Do not hard-code the brand across unrelated business rules; centralize it when an architecture is selected.

Goodly is an international catalog connecting customers with products and services created/provided by parents and caregivers caring for children with disabilities. Their work may provide primary or additional household income. The mission emphasizes opportunity, access to customers, and income through their own work.

The platform is free to use: no commissions, selling fees, or charges for sellers or buyers, and no profit from their listings or transactions. Transactions and service contact happen on external seller websites. Goodly does not process purchases or bookings.

Initial operator: Pavel as an individual. The operating country he stated for this project is the Netherlands. Full legal operator details, public contact email, domain, and legal disclosures remain to be finalized; do not infer them from another project or account profile.

## Users

| User | Capabilities |
| --- | --- |
| Visitor/buyer | Browse, search, filter, visit external listing links, view sellers, report a seller, use Contact |
| Seller applicant | Register, confirm email, complete and submit a seller profile |
| Approved seller | Publish listings, hide/show/delete eligible listings, request individual profile changes, communicate with administrator, request deletion |
| Blocked seller | Log in, see the reason, and send a text appeal; public profile/listings remain unavailable |
| Administrator | Review seller applications/profile changes, moderate sellers/listings, review private reports, manage Messages/Contact inquiries/categories/static translations/theme |

Pavel is the first and initially sole administrator. Public signup creates no administrator role. Buyers have no accounts.

## Launch scope

International from the first version, with 15 interface languages. English is the main language and source of static copy. User content is automatically translated according to `translations.md`.

The public site is the first product area to complete. Seller/admin functionality is required for the full MVP, but public browsing must not be delayed by adding unrelated marketplace features.

## Excluded features

No cart, checkout, payments, internal orders, service booking, buyer messaging/accounts, social login, seller directory, listing detail page, seller-created tags, product variants, currency conversion, review/rating system, background translation queue, automated external-link checker, or general page builder. No product return/exchange interface or listing fields. This absence does not define or remove any external seller's legal obligations.

## Present stage

Planning and documentation. No Goodly application repository/scaffold, database, executable environment, or hosting deployment has been verified. A read-only audit is being run on Pavel's Kali computer. The chat workspace is not his workstation.
